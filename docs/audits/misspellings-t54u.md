# Misspellings among the t53u insufficient-coverage groups (t54u)

Read-only. No grade was changed for this list.

The 1,252 insufficient-coverage groups in `docs/audits/source-weight-rescan-t53u.md` were checked for misspellings or typo variants of an ingredient that has an integrative position in that audit. A shared catalog word is not coverage. A group has coverage only when its audit reason cites a page that was read.

Spelling variants that the t53u audit already grouped with the correct spelling stay in that group. They are not a second group.

## Misspelling of a form that has integrative coverage

| Misspelling | Correct form | That form's coverage |
|---|---|---|
| Rheum Officinale (Rubarb) Root Extract | rhubarb (Rheum officinale root extract) | One source. MSK About Herbs, https://www.mskcc.org/cancer-care/integrative-medicine/herbs/rhubarb . Excess rhubarb is tied to kidney failure because it is high in oxalates. Mapped Caution for excess intake. Not applied as a grade change for this small root-extract token. Fewer than two sources, so the t53u result is no change. |
