Documented 4-tier scoring engine at docs/METHODOLOGY.md — the source of truth for all ratings (content now, scanner later).
Output: Clean / Caution / Avoid (no numeric score). Internal demerit count: limited = 1pt, moderate = 2pt; 3pts = Avoid; any high-risk additive = automatic Avoid; 0 = Clean.
HIGH-TIER PRINCIPLE (locked): high = strong evidence of a real harm mechanism (carcinogen, genotoxic, endocrine, organ/dev/neuro). A regulatory ban or IARC/NTP classification is sufficient but NOT required — robust science qualifies it even if the FDA permits it. Do NOT use "legal/GRAS" as a baseline for leniency. Weak/contested evidence keeps something out of high, not legality.
LEGAL GUARDRAIL: every Avoid rests on citable evidence; flags state facts, never "unsafe" (per the Genexa/NAD ruling).
Calibrations: PG = moderate (oral); aspartame = high (IARC 2B); sucralose/ace-K/saccharin = moderate; BHA + BHT = high; xylitol/erythritol = limited oral, cleared topical/nasal.
